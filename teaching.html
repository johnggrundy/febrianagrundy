Teaching

Purposeful, flexible, and reflective communication.

Febriana’s teaching begins with understanding learners’ experiences, goals, and challenges, and connects language learning to purposes that matter in their lives. She helps students become confident, independent communicators across academic, professional, and personal contexts.

Teaching dossier (PDF)CV

Teaching approach

Her courses emphasize authentic communication, structured guidance, targeted feedback, peer review, revision, and reflection. She uses corpus-based tools, including the Research Writing Tutor and AntConc, to help students investigate disciplinary writing conventions and make informed language choices.

She integrates technology when it serves a clear pedagogical purpose, using collaborative and multimodal tools and exploring responsible uses of generative AI. Her goal is to help students develop strategies they can transfer to future writing and communication tasks.

Teaching record

Febriana served as an Instructor of Record at Iowa State University from 2021 to 2026, independently teaching academic writing, composition, English grammar, and academic speaking. Her prior teaching experience in Indonesia included university courses in Teaching English as a Foreign Language methodology and academic writing, as well as General, Business, and Academic English.

Her teaching dossier reports a mean course-section evaluation of 4.59 out of 5.00 across 22 course sections at Iowa State University (2021–2026), with 432 course enrolments recorded in the dossier’s evaluation table.
