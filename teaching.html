Teaching

Purposeful, flexible, and reflective communication.

Febriana’s teaching is grounded in helping students become purposeful communicators who can adapt their language across audiences, genres, disciplines, and contexts.

Teaching portfolioCV

Teaching approach

Her courses emphasize process-oriented writing, scaffolded assignments, project-based learning, multimodal communication, peer review, reflection, and ethical engagement with technology and AI.

She makes academic communication practices explicit so students can transfer writing and communication strategies beyond a single course.

Teaching record

Febriana has taught undergraduate and graduate writing, speaking, grammar, ESL, and foundational communication courses, with university teaching experience at Iowa State University and prior teaching experience in Indonesia.

Her CV reports a total teaching evaluation average of 4.53 across 386 students.
